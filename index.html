Transparency

How to read this register

The dashboard separates official source fields from derived analysis and missing evidence.

Source data

Official work records

Descriptions, dates, amounts, status, MP, house and term are retained from eSAKSHI or the cited MPLADS export.

Evidence review

Images and PDFs

When attachments are available, the service stores them, displays them and compares their contents with the project record for human review.

Responsible analysis

Signals, not verdicts

AI output highlights possible inconsistencies. It is not proof of fraud and should be checked against official records and field evidence.
