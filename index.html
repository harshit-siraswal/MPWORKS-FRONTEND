Evidence stays legible.

Source first

Work text, administrative fields, dates, amounts and status are retained from the source export. No record is replaced with synthetic content.

02

Images are evidence

When the official source exposes an attachment, the backend can download it, record its hash and compare image fingerprints. Missing images remain “not in source”.
